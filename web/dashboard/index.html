wasted units is an upper bound on speculation's cost — a revoked worker stops early, so the real figure is lower. observed throughput is measured on the coordinator's clock, never from a worker's self-reported gpu_ms. correction is the EWMA of actual/predicted duration: 1.0 means the join-time benchmark has been accurate for that worker, and since the clamp is 10×, a value sitting exactly at 10 means the estimator has run out of room.
